# Startup credits you can claim without a VC

Headline amounts from each official page. "Up to $217K" = Microsoft $150K + PostHog $50K + Cloudflare $10K + AWS $5K + Google $2K. What you get on approval is closer to **$68K** (AWS $1K + Google $2K + Microsoft $5K + Cloudflare $10K + PostHog $50K).

## No investor needed
1. **Microsoft for Startups** — up to $150K in Azure credits. Up to $5K after business verification; more unlocks as your Azure usage grows. https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/startups
2. **PostHog for Startups** — $50K in credits. Company under 2 years old, under $5M raised, company email domain. https://posthog.com/startups
3. **Cloudflare for Startups (Tier 3)** — $10K for bootstrapped/self-funded companies with under $1M raised. Incorporated within 10 years, live website, LinkedIn/X/GitHub presence, first-time applicants. https://www.cloudflare.com/forstartups/
4. **AWS Activate Founders** — starts at $1K; select startups get up to $5K. Pre-Series B, founded in the last 10 years, paid-tier AWS account. https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits
5. **Google for Startups Cloud Program (Start tier)** — $2K. Founded in the last 2 years, working MVP, plans to raise soon, working website and matching business email. https://cloud.google.com/startup

## Also worth a look
- **Notion for Startups** — 3 months of Business with Notion AI without a partner (under 100 employees, website, company email; 1 month for teams under 10). https://www.notion.com/startups
- **MongoDB for Startups** — up to $15K Atlas credits, depending on stage and partner. https://www.mongodb.com/solutions/startups
- **Stripe Atlas** — $500 to incorporate; includes $2.5K Stripe credits and partner offers. https://stripe.com/atlas

## Needs a VC, accelerator or partner
AWS Activate Portfolio (up to $200K) · Google Cloud Scale/Scale AI ($200K–$350K) · Cloudflare Tier 2/1 ($100K/$350K) · Claude for Startups (up to $100K via partner VCs; current cohort full). DigitalOcean Hatch is paused until early 2027.

## How to apply well
1. Set up a real domain, a one-page site and a company email first.
2. Add a LinkedIn company page and a public GitHub org.
3. Apply to the free-tool credits you'll actually use this month; credits expire.
