# Paid summer programs for CS students (2027)

Five paid programs you can target before your 3rd year. Each was checked on its official page on 26 Nov 2026. Two close by 30 Nov.

## 1. Summer@EPFL (Switzerland)
- **Link:** https://summer.epfl.ch/
- **What:** a 2–3 month research internship in an EPFL lab, usually between May and September.
- **Pay:** a living allowance of CHF 1,800 a month (prorated to your actual dates) plus a refund of travel costs.
- **Who:** students enrolled in a BSc/BTech/MSc/MTech (or equivalent) who are in the 2nd year of a bachelor's or above. EPFL's own students and PhD students can't apply.
- **Deadline:** Sunday 29 November 2026, AoE (that's the afternoon of 30 Nov in India). Don't wait for the last hour.
- **How:** upload a CV, all university transcripts and a motivation letter (ideally 1 page) as PDFs, and pick up to 3 scientific domains. Recommendation letters aren't used.

## 2. Science Academies' Summer Research Fellowship (SRFP 2027)
- **Link:** https://webjapps.ias.ac.in/fellowship2027/
- **What:** two months of research with a scientist linked to IASc, INSA and NASI, any time in calendar year 2027.
- **Pay:** a monthly fellowship for living expenses plus round-trip train fare (the amount isn't on the page).
- **Who:** BE/BTech/BCA students in their **2nd and 3rd years only**.
- **Deadline:** 30 November 2026 (online).

## 3. Amazon Future Engineer (India)
- **Link:** https://press.aboutamazon.com/in/2026/5/amazon-future-engineer-program-expands-to-2-200-scholars-with-500-new-scholarships-for-women-in-computer-science
- **What:** a scholarship for women in B.E./B.Tech computer science or allied branches from low-income families.
- **You get:** ₹2 lakh over four years, a laptop, bootcamps, 1:1 mentorship from Amazon engineers and a **paid 8-week internship at Amazon after 2nd year**.
- **Dates:** the 2026 announcement (500 new scholarships) gives no application deadline. Ask your college's placement or scholarship cell.

## 4. Mitacs Globalink Research Internship (Canada)
- **Link:** https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/globalink-research-internship-students/
- **What:** a funded research internship in Canada. The award covers part of your airfare, a housing stipend, a food stipend and health insurance.
- **Who (India):** Indian nationals in B.E./B.Tech at a listed institution, 70%+, who have finished 2 years or are in 2nd year. IITs aren't eligible for 2027.
- **Status:** the summer 2027 call has closed, and no date for the next call is posted. Use this year to build a research CV.

## 5. Explore Microsoft
- **Link:** https://careers.microsoft.com/v2/global/en/exploremicrosoft
- **What:** an 8-week summer internship (India) for early-year students. You work in a small team on a real product.
- **Who:** the Explore page lists a 2nd-year application for India and says you don't need to be a CS major. You need an intro CS course and one semester of calculus. Note: Microsoft's separate eligibility page lists India Explore interns under pre-final year, so check the live posting.
- **Dates and pay:** no 2027 dates or pay details are posted yet.

## 1st year?
Most of these open from 2nd year. Use this year for one solid project, good grades (SRFP and Mitacs check marks) and a clean CV.
