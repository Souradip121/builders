# Free AI and dev tools for students in India (Nov 2026)

Total value at list prices: **₹60,000+**. Google AI Plus ₹4,788 (₹399 × 12) + Azure $100 + Heroku $312 ($13 × 24) + MongoDB $50 + Notion Plus $120/yr + Figma Professional $192/yr comes to about ₹77,000 at $1 = ₹93.41, before counting Copilot, 1Password, Datadog or JetBrains.

## Step 1: get verified on GitHub Education
https://education.github.com/pack · apply via your GitHub settings → Education benefits.
Use your college email or upload proof (student ID, transcript or letter). Your school may require its academic email. Approval can take a few days.

## 1. GitHub Copilot Student
Unlimited code completions, a GitHub AI Credits allowance, and limited chat and agent use, free while you're a verified student (rechecked monthly). Activate it under Education benefits. Docs: https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/manage-your-account/free-access-with-copilot-student

## 2. Google AI Plus, free for 1 year
https://gemini.google/in/students
For eligible university students aged 18+. Redeem by **31 December 2026**. A payment method is required, and it renews at ₹399/month unless you cancel. Verify eligibility every year.

## 3 to 7. Inside the Student Developer Pack
- **Microsoft Azure**: $100 credit + 25+ free services (18+)
- **Heroku**: $13/month credit for 24 months
- **MongoDB Atlas**: $50 in credits (+ MongoDB University access)
- **1Password**: free for 2 years, including Developer Tools
- **Datadog**: Pro account with 10 servers, free for 2 years
Also in the pack: $25 in Stripe credits, a free .TECH domain for 1 year, a .me domain for 1 year (Namecheap), 6 months of Frontend Masters.

## 8. Figma Professional
https://figma.com/education/apply · Students and educators get the Professional tier, including "full access to Figma's AI toolkit, including Figma Make". A Professional full seat lists at $16/mo.

## 9. Notion Plus
https://www.notion.com/pricing · The Plus plan (1 member) is free for students; your school email must be the account's main email. Notion AI is only a trial.

## 10. JetBrains IDEs
https://lp.jetbrains.com/pycharm-for-students · All JetBrains IDEs free for a year, renewable while you're a full-time student. Only the free AI tier is included.

## Not free for students in India right now
- ChatGPT's free Plus offer for students was US-only (deadline 31 Oct 2026).
- Cursor's student page has no student plan today.
- Claude: no free student plan; only through a university's Claude for Education deal.
- Canva: college students aren't eligible yet.
