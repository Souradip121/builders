# 6 websites every CS student should bookmark (hackathons, contests, prizes)

Six free-to-browse sites where Indian CS students find hackathons, coding contests, ML competitions and scholarships all year. Everything below was checked on 27 Nov 2026. Always read each event's own rules page before you register.

## 1. Devfolio: apply to hackathons
https://devfolio.co/hackathons
- What it lists: hackathons you apply to, online and offline. On 27 Nov it showed **20 hackathons open for applications**.
- Open now: **HackOdisha 6.0** (Webwiz, NIT Rourkela), 36 h, **online, 11–13 Dec 2026**, free to register, teams of 1–5, $1,620 in prizes: https://hackodisha-6a.devfolio.co/
- Also open: Winter of Code 6.0 (online, from 10 Jan), Techacks 5.0 (Rajpura, 15–16 Jan) and more.
- How to use: build one profile (GitHub, resume, past projects) once; applications reuse it.

## 2. Unstop: competitions, quizzes, scholarships
https://unstop.com
- What it lists: hackathons (/hackathons), competitions and challenges (/competitions), quizzes (/quiz), **scholarships** (/scholarships), internships and jobs. Unstop Pro is an optional paid tier.
- How to use: read eligibility first; many events are limited to engineering students in India or to one college.

## 3. MLH (Major League Hacking): student hackathons + free Hack Weeks
https://www.mlh.com/seasons/2027/events
- MLH says it supports 1,000+ events a year in 100 countries. The 2027 season page lists each event's dates, city and format.
- India on the list: **Techacks 5.0**, Rajpura, Punjab, 15–16 Jan 2027 (in person).
- **Global Hack Week** (https://ghw.mlh.com) is monthly and "100% free for anyone, anywhere". Next: **Open Source Week, 4–10 Dec 2026**.

## 4. Devpost: online hackathons with cash prizes
https://devpost.com/hackathons
- Hosts public hackathons run by companies and communities worldwide, many fully online. You sign up with a free account.
- How to use: open the **Rules** tab first. It has the eligibility (age, countries), the exact deadline with time zone and the judging criteria. Ship a short demo video.

## 5. Kaggle: data and ML competitions
https://www.kaggle.com/competitions
- Real datasets, public leaderboards, notebooks. Featured competitions pay cash.
- Open now: **Enveda CASMI 2026** (predict molecules from mass spectra). $50,000 prize pool across the top 5, free to enter, closes **14 Dec 2026**. Kaggle's listing gives **7 Dec** as the entry/team-merger deadline, so accept the rules before then: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/enveda-CASMI26-molecule-id-mass-spectra

## 6. clist.by: every coding contest in one list
https://clist.by
- Tracks contests from **Codeforces, CodeChef, LeetCode, AtCoder**, plus HackerRank, Topcoder, USACO, Kaggle and many more (https://clist.by/resources/).
- How to use: filter to the 3–4 sites you actually do, and check it before each week starts.

## The 10-minute Sunday routine
1. clist.by: pick 2 contests for the week and block the time.
2. Devfolio + Unstop: shortlist one hackathon and one scholarship or quiz; note the deadline.
3. MLH + Devpost: check for anything online this month.
4. Kaggle: open one competition and read its Overview + Data tabs.
5. Put every deadline in your calendar with a 2-day reminder.
