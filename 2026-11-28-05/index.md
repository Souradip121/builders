# 5 more free tools your college email unlocks (Nov 2026)

Part 3 of the free-for-students series. Every tool here is free with your college email address (JetBrains also takes an ISIC card or the GitHub Student Pack). No total value claimed: not every offer publishes a price.

## 1. JetBrains Student Pack
https://www.jetbrains.com/community/education/
- What you get: **IntelliJ IDEA Ultimate, PyCharm, WebStorm, CLion, ReSharper and every other JetBrains IDE and .NET tool**, free.
- Licence lasts 1 year; renew free every year while you're a student.
- Who: full-time students at an accredited college. Verify with your **college email**, an ISIC card, or link your **GitHub Student Developer Pack**.
- Not included: JetBrains AI Pro. Licence is for learning only, no commercial work.
- How: apply at jetbrains.com/shop/eform/students, confirm the email, then log in to the IDE with your JetBrains account.

## 2. Notion Education plan
https://www.notion.com/product/notion-for-education
- What you get: a free one-member workspace with unlimited pages and blocks, file uploads, more customisation and publishing options and 30-day version history.
- Who: students at an accredited college or university. **Only a school email works**: Notion does not accept student IDs. Personal Gmail doesn't qualify.
- How: make your college email your account's main email, then pick the Education plan in Settings. Re-verify once a year.
- Bonus: via the GitHub Student Pack, Notion adds extra AI responses on top of the Education plan.

## 3. Miro Student plan
https://miro.com/education/
- What you get: free for **2 years**: unlimited boards, a team of up to 10 members, unlimited external visitors, templates, core integrations and **100 Miro AI credits a month** per team.
- Who: students at an educational institution. Apply with your school email via "Apply as student"; reapply with proof of enrolment to extend.
- Use it for: system-design diagrams, hackathon planning, sprint boards for group projects.

## 4. Lucid (Lucidchart + Lucidspark)
https://lucid.co/lucidchart/education/students
- What you get: a free Lucid educational account with Lucidchart (diagrams, flowcharts, ER diagrams) and Lucidspark (a virtual whiteboard).
- Who: students in higher education with a school-issued email.
- How: sign up with your college email, choose **Student** in the first-login pop-up. Not granted? Use the request form in Lucid's help article.

## 5. Craft Education plan
https://www.craft.do/education
- What you get: **unlimited docs and up to 250 GB of storage** (the free Starter tier stops at 10 docs).
- Valid 1 year, unlimited renewals while you're enrolled.
- Who: currently enrolled students, faculty or staff. Craft checks that your account email belongs to an academic institution, so sign up (or switch) to your college email.

## The order
1. Add your college email to every account first.
2. Notion, Lucid and Craft verify through the email itself.
3. JetBrains: apply with the email (or GitHub Pack if your college domain isn't recognised).
4. Miro: submit the student form and wait for approval.
