# FREE vs PAID: 5 free swaps for apps students pay for

Paid prices are the regular prices on each official page (India pages where they show ₹), checked 25 Nov 2026. Adobe also runs first-year discounts; the regular price is what you pay after them. Every swap below is free to download, and four of the five are fully open source.

| Paid app (official price) | Free swap | Licence | What it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adobe Photoshop: ₹733.96/mo incl. GST (annual plan, billed monthly) | [GIMP](https://www.gimp.org/) | GPL-3.0 | Photo editing, retouching, compositing |
| Adobe Illustrator: ₹733.96/mo incl. GST (annual plan, billed monthly) | [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/) | GPL-3.0-or-later (binaries; most source GPL-2.0-or-later) | Vector graphics, logos, SVG |
| Adobe Premiere: ₹733.96/mo incl. GST (annual plan, billed monthly) | [Kdenlive](https://kdenlive.org/) | GPL-3.0 | Video editing on Linux, Windows, macOS, BSD |
| Microsoft 365 Personal: ₹689/mo or ₹6,899/yr | [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/) | MPL-2.0 | Docs, sheets, slides |
| Grammarly Pro: from $12/mo (USD) | [LanguageTool](https://languagetool.org/) | Core: LGPL-2.1 or later | Grammar and style checking |

Bonus: drawing or digital painting? [Krita](https://krita.org/) is GPL-3.0 and free.

## Notes on each
- **GIMP**: the closest free swap for Photoshop-style photo work. Learn layers, masks and the Paths tool first.
- **Inkscape**: SVG is its main format, so your logos and icons drop straight into websites.
- **Kdenlive**: from KDE. Its site lists Linux, Windows, macOS and BSD.
- **LibreOffice**: a full office suite "at no cost to you", from The Document Foundation. Writer is its word processor. If your college wants Word files, open your final file once and check the layout before you submit.
- **LanguageTool**: has a free Basic plan in the browser and works in Word and LibreOffice. Because the core is open source, you can also run your own server (see dev.languagetool.org/http-server).

## The student catch
Adobe's student Creative Cloud Pro plan is cheap in year one, then ₹699.01/mo incl. GST. Grammarly also has a free plan (spelling, grammar, tone, 100 AI prompts a month), so you may not need Pro at all.

## How to switch without pain
1. Install one swap this week, not all five.
2. Redo one real assignment or edit in it.
3. Save in open formats (PNG, SVG, ODT, MP4) so you're never locked in.
4. Cancel the paid plan only once the swap covers what you need.
