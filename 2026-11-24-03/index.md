# 6 places to launch your SaaS (that aren't Product Hunt)

Product Hunt is free to launch on, but it's one day. Stack these instead: one launch a week, each to a different crowd.

## 1. Show HN (Hacker News)
- Link: https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html (rules) · post via https://news.ycombinator.com/submit
- For something you've made that people can try. Title starts with "Show HN".
- Off topic: blog posts, sign-up pages, newsletters, landing pages. Don't ask friends to upvote.
- Cost: no fee mentioned on the rules page.

## 2. Peerlist Launchpad
- Link: https://peerlist.io/launchpad
- Weekly. Launches open Monday 12:00am to 11:59pm UTC (or schedule one) and run for a week.
- You need a verified individual profile and a 100%-complete project. Ranking uses upvotes, comments and traction.
- Top 3 get a badge and a newsletter spot; top 5 get a backlink.

## 3. DevHunt
- Link: https://devhunt.org
- Dev tools only, voted by developers (GitHub or Google login). Pick a launch week; most votes at week's end wins.
- Listing is free (nofollow link). A $49 launch adds a newsletter spot, a dofollow link and a tie-break.

## 4. Uneed
- Link: https://www.uneed.best
- Daily launches at 12:00 AM PST. The free line gives a date at least 50 days out.
- Paid: Fast-track $19.99, Skip the line $39.99, Relaunch $15. Top 3 each week go in the newsletter.

## 5. TinyLaunch
- Link: https://www.tinylaunch.com
- Weekly launches. Standard is free. Premium is $39 per launch (skips the queue, guaranteed backlink).
- Top 3 get a badge; the backlink is dofollow only if you embed their badge.

## 6. Fazier
- Link: https://fazier.com/submit
- Daily launches with weekly and monthly leaderboards.
- Free Basic: reviewed and listed within 30 days, and you must link back to Fazier. Lite $29, Premium $49.

## Bonus for students
Microlaunch (https://microlaunch.net) is paid (Launch Pro $49) but gives open-source projects and students 50% off.

## A 6-week plan
1. Week 1: Show HN, once people can use the product without signing up.
2. Week 2: Peerlist Launchpad (Monday).
3. Week 3: DevHunt, if it's a developer tool.
4. Weeks 4 to 6: TinyLaunch, Fazier and Uneed, booked early because of their queues.
5. After each one, write down where signups came from and which ones activated.
