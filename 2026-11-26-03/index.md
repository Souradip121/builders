# 5 Kaggle contests with $700K in cash prizes (open now)

Part 2 of the cash-contests list. All five are on Kaggle, free to enter, and open to residents worldwide (India included). The rules exclude only Crimea, DNR, LNR, Cuba, Iran, North Korea and people under US export controls. You must be 18 or the age of majority where you live, whichever is older. Teams of up to 5. All deadlines are **11:59 PM UTC**, which is **5:29 AM IST the next morning**. Prizes are in USD.

| Contest | What you build | Prizes | Key dates |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Google: Agent Harness on Physical-Engineering's Last Exam](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/physical-engineerings-last-exam) | An open-source agent harness paired with a Google-sponsored model, on 85 expert-reviewed engineering tasks | $400,000 (1st $250K, 2nd $100K, 3rd $50K) | Final submission 5 Jan 2027 |
| [Google Quantum AI: Verifiable Quantum Circuit Compilation](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/google-verifiable-quantum-circuit-compilation) | Compile quantum circuits using fewer resources | $150,000 (1st $50K; plus $30K single-task prizes, $10K longest leader) | Entry 13 Jan, final 20 Jan 2027 |
| [Santa 2026: The Elf Calculator](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/santa-2026) | The shortest +, -, x, / programs for 1,000 number puzzles | $50,000 (1st $12K, 2nd $10K, 3rd $10K, 4th $8K, $10K Rudolph Prize) | Entry 21 Jan, final 28 Jan 2027 |
| [Name That LLM](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/name-that-llm) | A classifier that identifies which model wrote a text | $50,000 (top 10 get $5K each) | Entry 6 Jan, final 13 Jan 2027 |
| [Generalist Gaming Agent](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/generalist-gaming-agent) | An agent that can play unfamiliar games | $50,000 (1st $16K, 2nd $12K, 3rd $9K, 4th $7K, 5th $6K) | Entry 22 Feb, final 1 Mar 2027 |

"Entry deadline" = the last day to accept the rules and join or merge a team. Miss it and you can't compete.

## Which one should you pick?
- **Like DSA and puzzles?** Santa 2026. No dataset to clean: it is pure search and optimisation, scored by total steps (lower is better).
- **Building with LLMs?** Name That LLM (10 equal prizes, so top 10 is a real target) or the Agent Harness hackathon (judged on a Kaggle Writeup plus a GitHub repo).
- **Physics / quantum curious?** The Google Quantum AI contest.
- **Want the most time?** Generalist Gaming Agent runs until March.

## How to start tonight
1. Create a Kaggle account and open the contest's **Rules** tab. Accept the rules (that's your entry).
2. Read the **Evaluation** and **Data** sections on the Overview page.
3. Fork a top public notebook from the **Code** tab, submit it once, then improve it.
4. Find teammates in the Discussion tab, before the team-merger deadline.
5. Winners have to share their solution (for example, Santa requires a write-up within 14 days), so keep notes as you go.
