# THIS IS GOLD: the summer 2027 internship application system

December to March, one folder at a time. Everything here is free.

## 01 · Resume (December)
- **Jake's Resume** on Overleaf (https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/jakes-resume/syzfjbzwjncs): a free, one-page LaTeX template (MIT License). Click "Open as Template", edit in the browser on a free Overleaf account.
- One page. Education, Projects, Experience, Skills. Put GitHub and LinkedIn links at the top.
- 2 projects, each with a number: users, requests/sec, time saved, dataset size, test coverage. Only real numbers.

## 02 · GitHub + LinkedIn (December)
- Pin your 2 best repos; each gets a README with a screenshot, a live link and setup steps.
- LinkedIn: headline like "CS @ <college> · building <thing>", same projects as your resume, "Open to work: internships".

## 03 · Where to look (January)
- **Company careers pages**: the source of truth. Bookmark 20 companies you'd like.
- **LinkedIn Jobs** (https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/): filter Job type = Internship, location = India; turn on job alerts.
- **Internshala** (https://internshala.com/): India's internship platform; free to apply (its FAQ says so), with a work-from-home filter.
- **Wellfound** (https://wellfound.com/): startup jobs; free for candidates, apply directly to founders and hiring managers.
- **Unstop** (https://unstop.com/): internships plus competitions, hackathons and scholarships for students.

## 04 · Referrals (January)
Find an engineer at the company (alumni first). Message template:
> Hi <name>, I'm a <year> CS student at <college>. I saw the <role> internship (<job ID/link>). I built <project, 1 line with a number>. Would you be open to referring me? Resume: <link>. Totally fine if not, thank you!

Keep it under 80 words. Always send the job link.

## 05 · Tracker
A free Google Sheet with columns: Company · Role · Link · Date applied · Source (careers/LinkedIn/referral) · Referrer · Status · Next step · Follow-up date · Notes.

## 06 · Apply + prep (February-March)
- Apply to 5 roles a week. Log each one the same day.
- Follow-up rule: no reply in 7 days, send one polite follow-up to your referrer or recruiter. Then move on.
- Online assessments: work through the **NeetCode 150** (https://neetcode.io/practice/practice/neetcode150), a curated list of 150 problems (Blind 75 + 75 more) with free video solutions.
- Practise explaining each project in 2 minutes.
