# 6 more free student perks your college ID unlocks (Nov 2026)

Part 2 of PERKS. None of these were in part 1. Each needs proof that you're a student: a college email, a student ID or an enrolment letter. All free; no total value claimed because not every offer publishes a price.

## 1. Unity Student
https://unity.com/products/unity-student
- What you get: the latest **Unity Pro Editor**, an Odin Inspector and Validator education licence, a premium Synty asset bundle and Unity Cloud access.
- Free for **1 year**, renewable every year while you're enrolled.
- Who: students **16+** at an accredited institution. Verification via SheerID, usually instant.

## 2. Autodesk Education plan
https://www.autodesk.com/in/education/edu-software/overview
- Free **1-year**, single-user access to Autodesk software, including **Fusion**. Renew yearly while eligible (from 30 days before expiry).
- Who: students at a qualified (government-accredited) institution. SheerID checks you; a **student ID**, fee receipt, transcript or college letter works. You get 14 days to upload.
- For learning and research only, not paid/commercial work.

## 3. Office 365 A1
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/education/products/office
- Free web versions of **Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and OneNote**, plus Publisher and Access (PC only).
- Who: students with an eligible school email. Your college may already have it switched on: try signing in at office.com with your college email first.

## 4 to 6. Inside the GitHub Student Developer Pack
https://education.github.com/pack · verify once in GitHub settings → Education benefits with your college email or a photo of your student ID.
- **Name.com**: a free domain on 25+ extensions such as **.dev, .app and .live**. Use a unique account address or it may be flagged as a duplicate. Great for your portfolio.
- **Educative**: **6 months** free, 70+ courses (https://www.educative.io/github-students), plus 30% off a subscription.
- **Termius**: all **Termius Pro and Team** features free while you're a student (https://termius.com/education). An SSH client that syncs your servers across laptop and phone.

## Bonus: no ID needed
**AWS Educate** (https://aws.amazon.com/education/awseducate/): free self-paced cloud training, hands-on labs and shareable digital badges. No credit card. Open from age 13 with just an email; the job board is 18+.

## How to claim in one evening
1. Get GitHub Education approved first (it can take a few days).
2. While you wait: Office 365 with your college email, then Unity and Autodesk through SheerID with a clear photo of your student ID.
3. Once the Pack is approved: claim the domain, Educative and Termius from the Pack page.
