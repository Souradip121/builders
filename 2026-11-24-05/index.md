# The first-100-users marketing playbook

A folder tree you can copy into Notion or your repo. Do the folders in order; each one feeds the next.

## 📂 01-positioning/
Write one line and test it on 5 people:
> **[Product] helps [specific person] [do the job] without [the painful alternative].**
- Who it's for: one role, one situation. "Indie SaaS founders before their first paying user" beats "developers".
- The pain, in their words (you'll get these from research).
- What they use today instead: a spreadsheet, a freelancer, doing nothing.
- Why you, not that: one reason, not five.

## 📂 02-research/
Talk to 10 people who have the problem. Questions that work:
1. "Tell me about the last time this happened."
2. "What did you try? What did it cost you in time or money?"
3. "What did you do after that?"
Avoid "Would you buy this?": people are polite. Copy their exact phrases into your landing page.

## 📂 03-channel/
- Pick the one place your 10 interviewees already spend time (a subreddit, a Discord, X, LinkedIn, Instagram).
- Post there for 30 days: what you built this week, what broke, what you learned.
- Reply to every comment and DM. Early users come from conversations, not impressions.

## 📂 04-launch/
- **Show HN** (https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html): only once people can try it without a sign-up wall; no landing pages.
- One weekly launch site after that: Peerlist Launchpad (opens Mondays, UTC), DevHunt (dev tools only, free listing), TinyLaunch (free standard launch), Fazier (free Basic, link-back required), Uneed (free line, 50+ days out).
- Email your waitlist the day before every launch.
- Relaunch when you ship something big.

## 📂 05-measure/
Track three numbers per source:
- **Signups**: how many came.
- **Activation**: how many reached the moment the product is useful (first project created, first invoice sent).
- **Week-2 retention**: how many came back the next week.
Double down on the source with the best activation, not the most signups.

## Templates
- Positioning line: see 01.
- Interview script: see 02.
- Weekly post: "This week I shipped ___. It broke when ___. Next week: ___."
