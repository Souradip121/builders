# 3 paid, remote open-source programs for 2027

All three are remote and pay a stipend in USD. Amounts below are from the official pages, checked 25 Nov 2026.

## 1. Google Summer of Code (GSoC)
https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com
- **Who:** 18+ at registration, a student *or* an open-source beginner, eligible to work in your country. You can't have been accepted into GSoC more than once before.
- **Stipend for India (2026 rates):** $750 (small, ~90 h), $1,500 (medium, ~175 h), $3,000 (large, ~350 h). https://developers.google.com/open-source/gsoc/help/student-stipends
- **When:** 2027 dates are not announced yet. For reference, the 2026 contributor application window was 16–31 March. Watch https://developers.google.com/open-source/gsoc/timeline
- **How:** pick 1–2 organisations, read their contribution guidelines and AI policy, make small PRs, then write a proposal with a weekly plan.

## 2. Outreachy
https://www.outreachy.org
- **Who:** 18+, for people who face underrepresentation, systemic bias or discrimination in tech where they live. Past Outreachy interns and **all past GSoC interns are not eligible**.
- **Stipend:** $7,000 USD total for a 3-month, 30 h/week remote internship.
- **For Indian students:** you count as northern hemisphere, so only the **May–August** cohort, and you need 42 consecutive days free of classes and exams during it.
- **When:** per Outreachy's yearly schedule, May-cohort initial applications usually open in **early February**, the contribution period runs mid-March to mid-April. The initial application can't be saved, so write your essays first. https://www.outreachy.org/docs/applicant/

## 3. LFX Mentorship (Linux Foundation)
https://mentorship.linuxfoundation.org
- **Who:** 18+ (or by the start date), able to commit consistent hours, eligible to work where you live. You don't have to be a student.
- **Stipend for India:** $1,300 USD total for programs starting on or after 1 July 2026, paid in two halves (midterm + graduation). Not every program is funded; the program page says so. https://mentorship.linuxfoundation.org/docs/stipends/stipend-amounts
- **When:** there's no single deadline. Each program lists its own term dates, so filter by "Accepting". Up to 3 applications at a time, one completed program per career.

## Order matters
If you're eligible for Outreachy, think before taking GSoC: a past GSoC internship makes you ineligible for Outreachy.

## Your Dec–Feb plan
1. Shortlist 2 orgs that use a language you know.
2. Get 1 small PR merged in each (docs and tests count).
3. Join their chat and ask specific questions.
4. Draft your proposal before the window opens.
