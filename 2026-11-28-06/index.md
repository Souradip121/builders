# The 7 GitHub Student Pack offers worth claiming (Nov 2026)

The Pack page lists 83 partner offers today. Most are niche or short trials. These 7 are the ones a CS student in India will actually use. Offer text below is as stated on education.github.com/pack on 27 Nov 2026; offers change, so check before you plan around one.

## First: get verified
Apply at https://github.com/settings/education/benefits ("Start an application").
- Be 13+ and enrolled in a degree or diploma programme.
- **Add and verify your college email** in GitHub settings before applying. Use it all lowercase, no plus-addressing. Once anyone from your college verifies with an academic email, everyone after must use one too.
- No college email? Upload a **clear photo** of your college ID, fee receipt, transcript or enrolment letter that **shows a current date**. Blurry or undated proof is the top rejection reason.
- One academic email = one account. You can't reuse it on a second account.
- Rejected? Fix the reason in the email and reapply from the same page.

## The 7
1. **GitHub Copilot Student**: free for verified students. Unlimited code completions, an allowance of GitHub AI Credits, limited chat and agent use, auto model selection. Turn it on in Copilot settings. Eligibility is rechecked monthly.
2. **GitHub Pro + Codespaces**: free GitHub Pro while you're a student, and Pro-level Codespaces: **180 compute hours and 20 GB storage a month** (vs 120 h / 15 GB on Free). A full cloud dev box from any laptop.
3. **A free domain** for your portfolio. Pick one: **Name.com**: a select free domain on 25+ extensions like .dev, .app, .live (use a unique account email). **Namecheap**: 1 year of a **.me** domain + 1 SSL certificate for 1 year. **.TECH**: one standard .tech domain free for 1 year.
4. **JetBrains**: a free subscription to all JetBrains IDEs (IntelliJ IDEA Ultimate, PyCharm, WebStorm...), renewed annually. https://www.jetbrains.com/community/education/
5. **Microsoft Azure for Students**: **$100 in Azure credit** + free access to 25+ services, no credit card. 18+ only (a smaller 13–17 offer exists). https://azure.microsoft.com/free/students/
6. **MongoDB**: **$50 in Atlas credits**, MongoDB Compass, MongoDB University, plus a free certification valued at **$150**.
7. **Frontend Masters**: **6 months free**, all courses and workshops. Best right before an internship hunt.

## Honourable mentions
Heroku ($13/month credit for 24 months), 1Password (free for 2 years), Educative (6 months), Termius (Pro + Team while a student), Datadog (Pro, 10 servers, 2 years).

## How to redeem
Pack page → find the partner → "Get access" / "Sign up". Most timed offers start only when you activate them, so claim Frontend Masters and Educative when you have time to use them, not on day one.
