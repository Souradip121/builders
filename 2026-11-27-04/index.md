# FREE vs PAID: placement prep that costs ₹0

Five things students pay for during placement prep, and the free swap for each. Paid prices are from each official page on 26 Nov 2026 (in US dollars; some were sale prices, and your checkout may show a different amount in INR).

## 1. DSA problem lists: NeetCode Pro vs NeetCode 150
- **Paid:** NeetCode Pro (https://neetcode.io/pro). One-Year Access showed **$95.20** (list $199) and Lifetime **$207.90** (list $599) on 26 Nov. Pro includes an in-browser editor for its curated problems.
- **Free:** the NeetCode roadmap and lists (https://neetcode.io/roadmap, https://neetcode.io/practice): Blind 75, NeetCode 150 and 250, grouped by pattern. Some problems are marked Pro.
- **How:** one pattern at a time (arrays → two pointers → sliding window → ...). Watch the video only after a 30-minute honest attempt, then solve it on LeetCode.

## 2. DSA course: TUF+ vs Striver's A2Z sheet
- **Paid:** TUF+ on takeUforward (https://takeuforward.org/pricing): Basic/Core/Max plans from **$69** (shown 40% off on 26 Nov). Plus adds videos, editorials and a planner.
- **Free:** Striver's A2Z DSA sheet (https://takeuforward.org/dsa/strivers-a2z-sheet-learn-dsa-a-to-z): 400+ practice problems and 50 theory lessons in 19 modules. The pricing page lists "Full sheet curriculum" as included in the free plan.
- **How:** do it in order. The page estimates about six months at a steady pace.

## 3. System design: paid courses vs the System Design Primer
- **Free:** System Design Primer (https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer): 379k stars, CC BY 4.0. Covers scalability, caching, load balancing, databases and worked interview questions, with Anki flashcard decks.
- **How:** read the "how to approach a system design interview" section first, then one worked problem a week. Use the Anki decks on the bus.

## 4. Mock interviews: Aced Pro vs Aced Practice (formerly Pramp)
- **Paid:** Aced, formerly Exponent (https://www.aced.io/upgrade): Pro **from $12/month** (shown as $79/month on the other billing option), Expert from $20/month.
- **Free:** Aced Practice (https://www.aced.io/practice): live 1:1 peer mock interviews where you take turns as interviewer. Free users get monthly credits. Types include DSA, system design and behavioral. Pramp's sessions have been hosted here since July 2024 (https://pramp.com/).
- **How:** book 2 a week, and take notes on the interviewer's side too: you learn what a good answer sounds like.

## 5. Resume: Overleaf Student vs Overleaf free + Jake's Resume
- **Paid:** Overleaf Student: **$13/month** or **$98/year** (https://www.overleaf.com/user/subscription/plans).
- **Free:** Overleaf's free plan ($0) plus Jake's Resume template (https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/jakes-resume/syzfjbzwjncs, MIT licence, source at https://github.com/jakegut/resume).
- **How:** "Open as Template", replace the sections, keep it to 1 page, put project and GitHub links at the top.
