# 5 more campus programs for your resume (part 2)

Part 1 covered Microsoft Student Ambassadors, GitHub Campus Experts, GDG on Campus, AWS Student Builder Groups and the MLH Fellowship. These 5 are new. All are free to apply. Some windows are closed right now, so the dates below tell you exactly when to be ready. Checked on the official pages on 27 Nov 2026.

## 1. Cursor Campus Leads
- **Link:** https://cursor.com/campus-leads
- **Who:** current university students. About 5 hours a week.
- **Perks:** 6 months of Cursor Ultra, early access to new features, a private Slack with the Cursor team, funding and resources for campus events, custom swag, travel reimbursement for one hackathon from a verified list, and an all-expenses-paid kick-off trip to Cursor's San Francisco office.
- **When:** runs twice a year (fall and spring). Applications are closed now and **open again in January**. Fill the interest form on the page to get notified.

## 2. Claude Builder Club (Claude Campus Programs)
- **Link:** https://claude.com/programs/campus
- **Who:** undergraduates lead a Claude Builder Club on campus (there are also master's and PhD tracks). Students anywhere in the world can apply. 18+ and work authorisation in your country of study.
- **Perks:** a **USD 3,600 cash stipend** (subject to program terms), API credits, workshops with Anthropic guest speakers, hackathons and demo nights.
- **When:** the page lists applications as open **1 Sept to 12 Sept**, reviewed on a rolling basis. The current program runs Sept 2026 to June 2027 and is closed for new applications.

## 3. Figma Campus Leaders
- **Link:** https://www.figma.com/blog/figma-deepens-investment-in-india/
- **What:** students become design leaders on their campus, host events and build design programming. Already on 60+ North American campuses; India is the first expansion outside North America.
- **When:** the first India cohort begins **January 2027**. Its applications closed on 7 Nov 2026, so follow Figma for the next intake.
- **Meanwhile:** Figma is free for students via https://www.figma.com/education/

## 4. Red Hat Academy Student Ambassador
- **Link:** https://access.redhat.com/community/learn/academy/rha-sa
- **Who:** students enrolled in (or who completed) a Red Hat Academy course at their college, nominated by their instructor.
- **Perks:** run events, a title for your resume and LinkedIn, networking with Red Hat staff, and chances at **free Red Hat certification exam vouchers** through the RHA Excellence Program.
- **How:** ask your Red Hat Academy instructor to nominate you. No fixed dates on the page.

## 5. Altium Student Ambassador
- **Link:** https://www.altium.com/education/students/ambassador
- **Who:** 2nd or 3rd-year undergraduate engineering students with an Altium Designer student licence. Piloting in India, the US and Canada. Best for ECE / hardware folks.
- **Perks:** 1-month remote training, certificate, LinkedIn badge, T-shirt, a Letter of Recommendation and mentorship from the Altium Education team.
- **How:** application form, short technical assessment, interview, then training. **Closed right now** with no reopening date posted; bookmark it.

## Make it count
Put numbers on the resume line: "Ran 3 workshops for 150 students" beats "Campus Lead".
