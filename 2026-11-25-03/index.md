# 6 online hackathons open now ($300K+ in prizes)

## For founders: Microsoft Imagine Cup 2027
No longer student-only. Open to startups that are a registered legal entity founded on or after 2 Nov 2024, below Series C, with entrants 18+. Applications close **28 Feb 2027**. Champion: $250,000 cash + $1M in startup credits; Top Student Startup: $25K cash + $250K credits. https://imaginecup.microsoft.com/en-us

## Backups if you finish early
- Reddit's Game of the Day Hackathon: closes 8 Dec 2026, $35,000 — https://redditgameofday2026.devpost.com/rules
- Deck the Pixels (Google with Adobe): closes 7 Dec 2026, $25,000 — https://deckthepixels.devpost.com/rules

## How to place
1. Read the judging criteria first and build to them.
2. Ship a tight 2-minute demo video; it's often the first thing judges see.
3. If the rules require the sponsor's API or tool, make it central, not a footnote.
4. Submit hours before the deadline.
