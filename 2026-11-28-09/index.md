# 5 programs open to 2nd-year CS students (plan for 2027)

You were told to wait for 3rd year. None of these five check your year of study: they check your age, your hours and your work. Dates below are from each official page; where the 2027 dates aren't out yet, last year's cycle is given so you know when to watch.

## 1. Google Summer of Code (GSoC)
https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/
- **Who:** 18+ at registration, eligible to work in your country, and a student or an open source beginner (regular contributors don't qualify).
- **What:** a paid open source project with a mentoring org: ~90 h (small), ~175 h (medium) or ~350 h (large). Not an internship: you're an independent contributor with a stipend.
- **India stipend (2026):** $750 small, $1,500 medium, $3,000 large.
- **Last cycle:** contributor applications 16–31 March 2026, coding from 25 May. The 2027 timeline isn't published yet: https://developers.google.com/open-source/gsoc/timeline
- **How:** pick 2 orgs now, read their ideas lists, land a small PR before March.

## 2. Outreachy
https://www.outreachy.org/
- **Who:** anyone who faces under-representation, systemic bias or discrimination in their country's tech industry. Students and non-students welcome, no year-of-study rule, 18+.
- **What:** a remote, paid internship in open source: **$7,000 USD** total, 30 hours a week.
- **Students in India** can only apply to the **May–August** cohort and need 42 consecutive days free of classes and exams.
- **Usual mid-year schedule:** initial applications open early February, due end of February; contribution period mid-March to mid-April; interns announced mid-May.

## 3. LFX Mentorship (Linux Foundation)
https://mentorship.linuxfoundation.org/
- **Who:** 18+, able to commit consistent hours for the full term. Up to 3 applications at a time; one mentorship per career.
- **What:** 12-week terms of scoped tasks with weekly mentor reviews. Many programs carry a stipend paid in milestones; amount varies by program and country, and not every program is funded.
- **Status today:** 0 programs accepting applications. Each program page lists its own term dates, so check back.

## 4. FOSSEE internships (IIT Bombay)
https://fossee.in/fossee-internships
- **Who:** students from any college, any degree, any UG or PG stage.
- **When:** semester-long internship, registration around Jan/Feb (work from March, online); summer fellowship, registration in March (from May; online, on-campus or hybrid); autumn semester internship, registration Aug/Sep.
- **Selection:** learn a FLOSS tool and finish screening tasks (2–4 weeks).
- **Note:** no stipend. You get an e-certificate and a link to your published work.

## 5. JPMorganChase Code for Good
https://www.jpmorganchase.com/careers/explore-opportunities/programs/tfsg-hackathons
- **Who:** students enrolled in a bachelor's degree. India is one of the five listed locations.
- **What:** you build tech for socially focused organisations with JPMorganChase technologists.
- **2026 India rules:** INR 17,000–25,000 prize for each member of the winning team(s).
- **When:** no 2027 India date published yet; the page shows only locations currently open, so check it monthly.

## Your December plan
1. Make a GitHub account you'd show a stranger.
2. Pick 2 GSoC orgs and 1 FOSSEE project area.
3. Fix one small issue in each before February.
