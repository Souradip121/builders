# DSA in 30 days, as one folder tree

A 30-day plan built from the free **NeetCode 150** list: 101 of its 150 problems, grouped by pattern, about 3–4 a day. Everything here is free. Finishing it won't guarantee an internship; it gives you the core patterns you'll be asked to explain out loud.

## The free resources
- **NeetCode roadmap + NeetCode 150** (https://neetcode.io/roadmap, https://neetcode.io/practice): 18 patterns in order, from Arrays & Hashing to Math & Geometry. All 150 problems in the NeetCode 150 list are free (none marked Pro when checked), each with a video solution. NeetCode Pro is a paid add-on you don't need for this plan.
- **Striver's A2Z DSA sheet** (https://takeuforward.org/strivers-a2z-dsa-course/strivers-a2z-dsa-course-sheet-2): 400+ practice problems and 50 theory lessons by topic. The sheet is free to use; takeUforward's paid Plus adds extras. Use it when a topic needs more reps or theory.
- **LeetCode** (https://leetcode.com/problemset/): a free account is enough to submit every problem here. Premium is paid and not needed.
- **CS50x 2026** (https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/): Harvard's intro course, free via OpenCourseWare. Weeks 2–5 (arrays, algorithms, memory, data structures) if your basics feel shaky. Do it before Day 1.

## The tree (NeetCode 150 problem counts)
```
dsa-30-days/
├── week-1_days-01-07/   arrays & hashing (9) · two pointers (5) · sliding window (6)
├── week-2_days-08-14/   stack (6) · binary search (7) · linked list (11)
├── week-3_days-15-21/   trees (15) · heap / priority queue (7) · backtracking (10)
└── week-4_days-22-30/   graphs (13) · 1-D DP (12) · 2 timed mocks
```
Done when: week 1, you can spot "hash map" vs "two pointers" in under a minute. Week 2, you can write binary search from memory without off-by-one bugs. Week 3, you can do DFS on a tree both recursively and iteratively. Week 4, you solve 2 unseen mediums in 45 min each.

## The daily loop (1–2 hours)
1. Try the problem for 25 minutes. No hints.
2. Stuck? Watch the NeetCode video only until the idea clicks, then close it and code it yourself.
3. Write one line in a notes file: the pattern + the trick.
4. Re-solve yesterday's hardest problem from scratch.

## The 2 mocks (Day 29 and 30)
Pick 2 unseen mediums from the remaining NeetCode 150 topics, set a 45-minute timer each, and explain your approach out loud (to a friend, or record yourself) before you code.

## After Day 30
Finish the rest of NeetCode 150: Tries, Intervals, Greedy, Advanced Graphs, 2-D DP, Bit Manipulation, Math & Geometry.
